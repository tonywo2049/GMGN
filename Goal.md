# M1 Goal

## Close outcomes

1. M1-allocated agent, dispatch, tool, shell, and observed-process resources and interactions
   are attributable through end-to-end correlated collection. Sources:
   [M1 outcome](ROADMAP.md#outcome-and-allocation-value),
   [M1 deliverable 1](ROADMAP.md#deliverables), [M1 success signal](ROADMAP.md#success-signal),
   [D-001](Decision.md), and [D-003](Decision.md).
2. Linux and macOS `exec_command` process observation meets the fidelity already decided for
   each platform, every run exposes its observation quality, and unknown omissions remain
   unknown. Sources: [M1 deliverable 2](ROADMAP.md#deliverables),
   [M1 success signal](ROADMAP.md#success-signal), and [D-004](Decision.md).
3. Reporting distinguishes the resources and interactions allocated by M1 and exposes data
   quality, collection health, and telemetry overhead; collection and telemetry failure
   conform to their applicable decisions. Sources: [M1 deliverable 3](ROADMAP.md#deliverables),
   [M1 success signal](ROADMAP.md#success-signal), [D-002](Decision.md), and
   [D-005](Decision.md).
